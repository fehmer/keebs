## Highlights

I ordered this keyboard at the end of 2020 after giving up on a group buy for a similar-style keyboard that I joined a long time ago. While waiting for this GB to complete, I switched to ortholinear layouts. Fortunately, there were still some extras available, so I ordered [another one](/post/2022-06-05-saturn-60o) with an ortholinear layout. My girlfriend happily took over this one.

The Israfel is a brick-built, fixed split ergonomic keyboard. I got gifted this one for my birthday.

## Facts

|                      |                                              |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Model no.**        | Israfel                                      |
| **Manufacturer**     | KBDcraft                                     |
| **Manufacture Date** | 2025                                         |
| **Case**             | plastic, lego-compatible interlocking bricks |
| **Plate**            | FR4, gasket mount                            |
| **Switches**         | N² land mine, Choc v2 tactile                |
| **Keycaps**          | KBDcraft Keycap LB White on Black            |
| **Dimensions**       | 320 × 115 × 30 mm                            |
| **Weight**           | 360 grams                                    |
| **PCB/Software**     | Core56, QMK/Vial                             |

## Highlights

Fun little keyboard with surprisingly good quality. The build was fun, the keycaps are double-shot and have decent design, especially when you consider the price is around 13€. The LEGO knock-off bricks are not the best quality, especially the tiles on top of the board.

The switches and typing feel is very nice. It uses tactile Choc V2 low-profile switches. This is the first time I encountered Choc V2 and I quite like them. They are much nicer than the V1 (that said, I only have a few V1s to compare them with) and use the same MX-style cross mount so you can re-use your existing keycaps.

Overall, the price is very impressive for what you get. The PCB even has per-key RGB if you are into that.
